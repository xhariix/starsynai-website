AI Training for Teams

Corporate AI Training Your Team
Actually Uses on Monday

We run hands-on AI and automation sessions inside companies, studios and production houses. Every programme is built around what you ask for — you tell us the topics, the tools and the depth, and we shape the sessions to your team. No slide decks about the future of AI; your people bring their real work and leave with workflows already running.

Why Most AI Rollouts Stall

Everyone Has Access to AI.
Almost Nobody Uses It Well.

Buying licences is the easy part. The gap is between having the tool open and knowing what to do with it on a Tuesday afternoon with a deadline.

Everyone works differently

Two people on the same team get wildly different results from the same tool, because nobody agreed on how the work should actually be done.

Generic training doesn’t stick

A two-hour webinar on “prompting” is forgotten by Friday. Nothing changes because nothing was built during the session.

Nobody can prove the return

Without a baseline and a named workflow, leadership has no way to tell whether the AI budget bought anything at all.

The Method

How a Programme Runs

Four stages, in this order, every time. We never arrive with a fixed deck — we find out what your week actually looks like first, then design around it.

Stage 01

We Understand
Your Needs

A short discovery with your leads and the people doing the work — before anyone opens a tool. Where do hours actually disappear, what has been tried already, what data can and cannot leave the building.

You get: a written list of the workflows worth attacking first, with the effort and the payback beside each one.
Stage 02

We Design the
AI System

We architect the actual system for those workflows — the tools, the prompts, the automations, the human checkpoints and the guardrails. Built on the stack you already pay for wherever it will do the job.

You get: the design in writing — what gets automated, what stays human, what it will cost to run.
Stage 03

We Make Your Team
Proficient

Live, hands-on sessions where your people build the system themselves rather than watch slides about it. They keep going until they can run it, break it, fix it and explain it to a colleague without us in the room.

You get: working automations, role-specific prompt libraries, and a written playbook your team owns.
Stage 04

Client Satisfaction
& Handover

We measure the before and after on the workflows we picked in stage one, name an owner inside your team for each one, and stay reachable while it meets real deadlines for the first time.

You get: a measured result, a named owner per workflow, and a support window after handover.

Nothing gets built before stage one is signed off, and nothing gets called finished until stage four has a number against it. If a workflow turns out not to be worth automating, we will tell you that instead of building it.

Questions

Before You Book

Do our people need any technical background?+
No. Sessions are built around the work, not the technology. If someone can use email and a spreadsheet, they can follow along. Where we do touch automation tools like n8n, we set it up together on screen.
How long does a programme take?+
Anything from a single half-day session for one department up to a multi-week rollout across a whole company. Most teams start with a short pilot on one department, then expand once they can see the time saved.
Can you run it on-site?+
Yes. We deliver on-site or fully remote, and mixed formats work well — an on-site kick-off with remote follow-up sessions is a common shape.
Do you use our existing tools or sell us new ones?+
We start with what you already pay for. If a new tool is genuinely worth it we will say so and explain the cost, but the goal is to get more out of your current stack first.
What happens to sensitive company data?+
We agree the boundaries before the first session: what can go into which tool, what stays internal, and which steps always need a human review. That becomes part of the written playbook your team keeps.
What does it cost?+
It depends on team size, format and how many departments are involved. Tell us the shape of your team on a short call and you will get a written quote — no obligation.
